  • The effect size is how many standard deviations the means are apart.

    • An effect size of 1 means that the differences in means are 1 SD apart.
    • Basically: the SD becomes the new unit

  • It is a measure of signal strength, so it does not depend on sample size.

  • To calculate, use:

    • (mean(x1) - mean(x2)) / sd(x2)

  • You can get the means and SD from from your summary table like this (make sure to replace the names with the appropriate values)

    summary_table[row_number,column_number]

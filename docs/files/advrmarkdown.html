To create tables with pages, use the following in your yaml.

---
title: Lab
author: Your Name
output:
    html_document:
        df_print: paged
---

To create nice tables without pages, use the following in your yaml.

---
title: Lab
author: Your Name
output:
    html_document:
        df_print: kable

Use `r ` to use r code in your paragraphs so you can reference variables from you code chunks. For example:

The the CIs for groups one and two are `r CIs[1,2]` and `r CIs[2,2]`, respectively.

The the CIs for groups one and two are [63.78, 63.86] and [68.42, 68.51], respectively.

Note that [1,2] stands for row 1 and column 2 of the CI table, and [2,2] stands for row 2 and column 2 of the CI table.

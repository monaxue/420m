Teaching philosophy

  • I believe that the best way to learn involves 1) solving problems on your own, 2) discussing problems with peers, 3) asking for help.
  • Therefore, I have structured the lab section so you can
    • Code independenty, instead of just copying what I do
    • Discuss questions with your peers
    • Get direct feedback from me on your deliverables
  • Because of the way I structure class, class usually takes up the full two hours.

R installation and setup

  1. Install both R and R RStudio: https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/
  2. In the console of RStudio, run these
    • install.packages("tidyverse")
    • install.packages("ggplot2")
  3. Run the RMarkdown file, test.Rmd, to see if it’s working.
    1. Go to https://monaxue.github.io/420m/ and download test.Rmd. Then open it in Rstudio.
    2. Click on the “Knit” button
  4. When your RMarkdown file has successfully generated a .html file, let me know, and I will come check that it was successful. After you have shown me your file, you are free to leave.
